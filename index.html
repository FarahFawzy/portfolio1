Projects

ThermoPilot: decision support for central cooling

Team graduation project at the Administrative Capital for Urban Development (ACUD): a data-driven system that helps operators detect faults in chiller and water systems and choose how to respond.

What I built

  • Anomaly detection on simulated chiller and water-system data (24 files, 77 sensors, 12.6M minute-level rows aggregated to 210,216 hourly rows), using a normal-behavior model over 71 sensor targets with residual scoring and causal smoothing.
  • A time-based train/calibration/test split to prevent data leakage.
  • 31,500 simulations (1,500 fault scenarios × 21 response actions) to compare response policies.
  • Role-based dashboards for operators, engineers, and managers, plus a RAG and DuckDB/SQL data assistant that answers with retrieved passages, queries, and caveats.
  • A handover package (verification command, 16 new tests, hash-checked result files) and a phased shadow-mode pilot plan.

Results

  • 0.824 AUC with zero false-alarm episodes across 2,206 normal test hours.
  • Starting the response after 5 minutes instead of 30 cut the mean cooling deficit from 677.84 to 261.02 TRh (−59.62% standardized penalty, simulation only).
  • Honest about limits: strongest on bypass faults (100% of fault hours alarmed), weaker on fouling and cooling-tower faults.

ANKH: AI-powered Egyptian heritage platform

Graduation project (Digilians): a multimodal AI platform for exploring Egyptian cultural heritage using computer vision, NLP, and generative AI.

What I built

  • Hieroglyph analysis pipeline: 767-class symbol detection (YOLOv8/YOLO11), EfficientNet-B4 classification, transformer-based reading-order reconstruction, then English and Arabic translation via GPT-4o and Llama 3.
  • Multimodal RAG: identify an artifact or landmark from an image, retrieve historical knowledge, and generate personalized descriptions, narratives, and audio.
  • Document intelligence: text, table, and visual extraction with automated summarization and Visual Q&A.
  • A multilingual assistant for source-grounded Q&A, and a geolocation-based recommendation engine combining proximity scoring, LLM validation, and rule-based filtering.

Results

  • 92.5% accuracy on 767-class hieroglyph classification.
